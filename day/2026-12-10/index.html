Days 28–54: prayed in thanksgiving. Luminous Mysteries.

Opening

The Sign of the Cross

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, Amen.

Novena Opening Offering (in Thanksgiving)

Hail, Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, my Mother Mary, hail! At thy feet I gratefully kneel to offer thee a Crown of Roses, bright-yellow roses to remind thee of the light of thy Son's public life, each bud recalling to thee a holy mystery; each ten bound together with my thanksgiving for the favor I place in your hands. O Holy Queen, dispenser of God's graces, and Mother of all who invoke thee! Thou canst not look upon my gift and fail to see its binding. As thou receivest my gift, so wilt thou receive my thanksgiving; from thy bounty thou hast given me the favor I so earnestly and trustingly sought. I despaired not of what I asked of thee, and thou hast truly shown thyself my Mother!

PRAYER OF INTENTIONS

Lord Jesus, through the hands of Your Blessed Mother, we offer this Rosary for these intentions — for all of us lost sheep together:

  • For all of us lost sheep, that our work and creative callings, including this channel, may bear fruit, and that each of us may find a fulfilling, steady career.
  • For all our vocations — to the priesthood, religious life, or marriage — and for the safety and holiness of our future spouses and families.
  • For our stream family, all our lost sheep, guests, fans, and viewers, and every private intention we carry, that we may grow in holiness and be protected.
  • For all of us, that we and our loved ones may receive healing in body and mind, and for good health for our friends, families, and all who watch with us.
  • For all of us: for healing, patience, fortitude, humility, and purity.
  • For all of us who are suffering, struggling with addiction, despairing, or in need of hope — that we may grow in freedom and purity of heart.
  • For all of us who are far from God, and for those of other faiths, that we may return to Christ and His Church and know Jesus personally.
  • For our enemies and all who speak against us, that they may come to love Jesus, and that we may love them without hatred.
  • For all of us, for discernment and freedom from all spiritual deception, that God in grace and mercy make the truth abundantly clear.
  • For protection and clarity for all of us to recognize bad advice, whether it comes from people or from AI.
  • For all of us, for the repose of the souls of our beloved dead.
  • For all of us, that peace and charity may grow in our families and homes.
  • For all of us, for our priests, bishops, and all who serve the Church.
  • For all of us, that we may clearly discern our vocations and future paths, and find peace amid anxiety, grief, and heavy burdens.
  • For all of us, that we may have what we need and be free from crushing financial worry, and have strength in our studies.
  • For all of us, for an end to suffering.
  • For all of us, for an end to pain.
  • For all of us, for an end to the feeling of loneliness.
  • For all of us, that we may be not just content but happy, and enjoy our lives while we wait to find our vocation.
  • For all of us, in thanksgiving for prayers answered and blessings received — and for every lost sheep watching and praying with us tonight.

And in thanksgiving for every blessing You have already given to all of us, and for those who guide and care for us.

When I called upon him, the God of my justice heeded me. In tribulation, you have enlarged me. Have mercy on me, and heed my prayer. The Lord will heed me when I cry out to him. Be angry, and do not be willing to sin. The things that you say in your hearts: be sorry for them on your beds. — Offer the sacrifice of justice, and hope in the Lord. Scripture · Leviticus 6 (CPDV)

Read Leviticus 6 in the NABRE →

1The Lord spoke to Moses, saying: 2The soul who will have sinned, and, despising the Lord, will have denied to his neighbor the deposit which he had entrusted to his safekeeping, or who will have extorted anything by force, or who will have made a false accusation, 3or who will have found a lost thing and then also withheld it by swearing falsely, or who will have done any other of the many things by which men usually sin: 4being convicted of the offense, he shall restore 5all that he wanted to obtain by fraud, the whole plus an additional fifth part, to the owner against whom he brought the damage. 6Then, on behalf of his sin, he shall offer an immaculate ram from the flock, and he shall give it to the priest, according to the estimation and measure of the offense. 7And he shall pray for him in the sight of the Lord, and he shall be released from any one of those things that he did when he sinned. 8And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: 9Instruct Aaron and his sons: This is the law of a holocaust. It shall be burned upon the altar, all night until morning. The fire shall be from the same altar. 10The priest shall be vested with the tunic and the linen undergarments. And he shall take up the ashes of that which the devouring fire has consumed, and, placing them next to the altar, 11he shall strip off his former vestments, and being clothed with others, he shall carry them beyond the camp, and he shall cause them to be consumed, even to glowing embers, in a very clean place. 12But the fire on the altar shall burn always, for the priest shall nourish it by placing wood under it each day in the morning. And, laying down the holocaust, he shall burn the fat of the peace offerings upon it. 13This is the perpetual fire which shall never fail upon the altar. 14This is the law of the sacrifice and the libations, which the sons of Aaron shall offer in the sight of the Lord, and before the altar. 15The priest shall take a handful of fine wheat flour, which has been sprinkled with oil, and all the frankincense, which has been placed upon the flour, and he shall burn it upon the altar as a memorial of most sweet odor to the Lord. 16And the remaining portion of the flour, Aaron shall eat with his sons, without leaven. And he shall eat it in the holy place, in the atrium of the tabernacle. 17Yet for this reason, it shall not be leavened, because part of it is offered as an incense of the Lord. The Holy of holies shall it be, just as what is offered on behalf of sin and of transgression. 18Only the males of the stock of Aaron shall eat it. This shall be an everlasting ordinance in your generations concerning the sacrifices of the Lord. All who will touch these shall be sanctified. 19And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: 20This is the oblation of Aaron and of his sons, which they must offer to the Lord in the day of their anointing. They shall offer a tenth part of an ephah of fine wheat flour as a perpetual sacrifice, half of it in the morning, and half of it in the evening. 21It shall be sprinkled with oil and fried in a frying pan. Then it shall be offered hot, as a most sweet odor to the Lord, 22by the priest who by law succeeds his father. And it shall be entirely burned on the altar. 23For every sacrifice of the priest shall be consumed by fire; neither shall anyone eat from it. 24Then the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: 25Say to Aaron and his sons: This is the law of the victim for sin. In the place where the holocaust is offered, it shall be immolated in the sight of the Lord. It is the Holy of holies. 26The priest who offers it shall eat it in the holy place, in the atrium of the tabernacle. 27Whatever will touch its flesh shall be sanctified. If a garment will be sprinkled with its blood, it shall be washed in a holy place. 28Then the earthen vessel, in which it was soaked, shall be broken. But if the vessel will be of brass, it shall be scoured and washed with water. 29Every male of priestly descent shall feed on its flesh, because it is the Holy of holies. 30For the victim that is slain for sin, whose blood is carried into the tabernacle of the testimony, for expiation in the Sanctuary, shall not be eaten, but it shall be consumed by fire.

Trivia · Leviticus 6
  • Someone who denies a deposit, takes anything by force, accuses falsely, or swears falsely about something he found restores all of it plus a fifth part, and he offers an immaculate ram.
  • The law of the holocaust: it burns on the altar all night until morning, and the fire on the altar never fails. Each morning the priest places wood under it and burns the fat of the peace offerings.
  • Aaron and his sons eat the remaining fine flour without leaven in the holy place. A sin offering whose blood is carried into the tabernacle of the testimony is not eaten. It is consumed by fire.
