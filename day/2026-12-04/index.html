The Sign of the Cross

Novena Opening Offering (in Thanksgiving)

Hail, Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, my Mother Mary, hail! At thy feet I gratefully kneel to offer thee a Crown of Roses, blood-red roses to remind thee of the passion of thy divine Son, with Whom thou didst so completely share each torment, each bud recalling to thee a holy mystery; each ten bound together with my thanksgiving for the favor I place in your hands. O Holy Queen, dispenser of God's graces, and Mother of all who invoke thee! Thou canst not look upon my gift and fail to see its binding. As thou receivest my gift, so wilt thou receive my thanksgiving; from thy bounty thou hast given me the favor I so earnestly and trustingly sought. I despaired not of what I asked of thee, and thou hast truly shown thyself my Mother!

PRAYER OF INTENTIONS

Lord Jesus, through the hands of Your Blessed Mother, we offer this Rosary for these intentions — for all of us lost sheep together:

  • For all of us lost sheep, that our work and creative callings, including this channel, may bear fruit, and that each of us may find a fulfilling, steady career.
  • For all our vocations — to the priesthood, religious life, or marriage — and for the safety and holiness of our future spouses and families.
  • For our stream family, all our lost sheep, guests, fans, and viewers, and every private intention we carry, that we may grow in holiness and be protected.
  • For all of us, that we and our loved ones may receive healing in body and mind, and for good health for our friends, families, and all who watch with us.
  • For all of us: for healing, patience, fortitude, humility, and purity.
  • For all of us who are suffering, struggling with addiction, despairing, or in need of hope — that we may grow in freedom and purity of heart.
  • For all of us who are far from God, and for those of other faiths, that we may return to Christ and His Church and know Jesus personally.
  • For our enemies and all who speak against us, that they may come to love Jesus, and that we may love them without hatred.
  • For all of us, for discernment and freedom from all spiritual deception, that God in grace and mercy make the truth abundantly clear.
  • For protection and clarity for all of us to recognize bad advice, whether it comes from people or from AI.
  • For all of us, for the repose of the souls of our beloved dead.
  • For all of us, that peace and charity may grow in our families and homes.
  • For all of us, for our priests, bishops, and all who serve the Church.
  • For all of us, that we may clearly discern our vocations and future paths, and find peace amid anxiety, grief, and heavy burdens.
  • For all of us, that we may have what we need and be free from crushing financial worry, and have strength in our studies.
  • For all of us, for an end to suffering.
  • For all of us, for an end to pain.
  • For all of us, for an end to the feeling of loneliness.
  • For all of us, that we may be not just content but happy, and enjoy our lives while we wait to find our vocation.
  • For all of us, in thanksgiving for prayers answered and blessings received — and for every lost sheep watching and praying with us tonight.

And in thanksgiving for every blessing You have already given to all of us, and for those who guide and care for us.

Fruit: Uniformity With the Will of God · Matthew 26:36

My Jesus, I believe that Thou art present in the Most Holy Sacrament. I love Thee above all things, and I desire to receive Thee into my soul. Psalm 4 (Vulgate 4)

When I called upon him, the God of my justice heeded me. In tribulation, you have enlarged me. Have mercy on me, and heed my prayer. Sons of men, how long will you be dull in heart, so that whatever you love is in vain, and whatever you seek is false? — And know this: the Lord has made wondrous his holy one. The Lord will heed me when I cry out to him. Be angry, and do not be willing to sin. The things that you say in your hearts: be sorry for them on your beds. — Offer the sacrifice of justice, and hope in the Lord. Many say, “Who reveals to us what is good?” The light of your countenance, Lord, has been sealed upon us. You have given joy to my heart. By the fruit of their grain, wine, and oil, they have been multiplied. In peace itself, I will sleep and I will rest. For you, O Lord, have established me singularly in hope. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

Psalm 90 (modern 91)

Whoever dwells with the assistance of the Most High will abide in the protection of the God of heaven. He will say to the Lord, “You are my supporter and my refuge.” My God, I will hope in him. For he has freed me from the snare of those who go hunting, and from the harsh word. He will overshadow you with his shoulders, and you will hope under his wings. His truth will surround you with a shield. You will not be afraid: before the terror of the night, before the arrow flying in the day, before the troubles that wander in the darkness, nor of invasion and the midday demon. A thousand will fall before your side and ten thousand before your right hand. Yet it will not draw near you. So then, truly, you will consider with your eyes, and you will see the retribution of sinners. For you, O Lord, are my hope. You have set the Most High as your refuge. Disaster will not draw near to you, and the scourge will not approach your tabernacle. For he has given his Angels charge over you, so as to preserve you in all your ways. With their hands, they will carry you, lest you hurt your foot against a stone. You will walk over the asp and the king serpent, and you will trample the lion and the dragon. Because he has hoped in me, I will free him. I will protect him because he has known my name. He will cry out to me, and I will heed him. I am with him in tribulation. I will rescue him, and I will glorify him. I will fill him with length of days. And I will reveal to him my salvation. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

Surrender Novena (Don Dolindo Ruotolo) · Day 4 of 9

Day 4

You see evil growing instead of weakening? Do not worry. Close your eyes and say to me with faith: “Thy will be done, You take care of it.” I say to you that I will take care of it, and that I will intervene as does a doctor and I will accomplish miracles when they are needed. Do you see that the sick person is getting worse? Do not be upset, but close your eyes and say, “You take care of it.” I say to you that I will take care of it, and that there is no medicine more powerful than my loving intervention. By my love, I promise this to you.

Ten times

O Jesus, I surrender myself to you, take care of everything!

To Our Lady

Mother, I am yours now and forever. Through you and with you I always want to belong completely to Jesus.

Trivia · Exodus 40
  • On the first day of the first month, Moses raises the tabernacle, places the testimony in the ark, draws the veil before it, and sets the bread of the presence and the seven lamps in order.
  • He anoints the tabernacle and its vessels with the oil of unction, washes Aaron and his sons, and clothes them in the holy vestments so that their unction may accomplish an everlasting priesthood.
  • The cloud covers the tabernacle of the testimony, and the glory of the Lord fills it, so that Moses cannot enter. When the cloud departs, the sons of Israel set out. The cloud is over it by day, and the fire by night.
