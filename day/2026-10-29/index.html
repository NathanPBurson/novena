Days 28–54: prayed in thanksgiving. Luminous Mysteries.

Opening

The Sign of the Cross

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, Amen.

Novena Opening Offering (in Thanksgiving)

Hail, Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, my Mother Mary, hail! At thy feet I gratefully kneel to offer thee a Crown of Roses, bright-yellow roses to remind thee of the light of thy Son's public life, each bud recalling to thee a holy mystery; each ten bound together with my thanksgiving for the favor I place in your hands. O Holy Queen, dispenser of God's graces, and Mother of all who invoke thee! Thou canst not look upon my gift and fail to see its binding. As thou receivest my gift, so wilt thou receive my thanksgiving; from thy bounty thou hast given me the favor I so earnestly and trustingly sought. I despaired not of what I asked of thee, and thou hast truly shown thyself my Mother!

PRAYER OF INTENTIONS

Lord Jesus, through the hands of Your Blessed Mother, I offer this Rosary for these intentions:

  • For this channel, that it may grow, bear good fruit, and become a way for me to serve You full-time.
  • For all our vocations, to the priesthood, religious life, or marriage, and for the safety and holiness of our future spouses and families.
  • For our stream family, all our lost sheep, and every viewer's private intentions, that they may grow in holiness and be protected.
  • For the sick, and for good health for our friends, families, and viewers.
  • For myself: for healing, for patience, fortitude, humility, and purity, and for a good, steady job.
  • For all who are suffering, struggling with addiction, despairing, or in need of hope.
  • For all who are far from God, and for those of other faiths, that they may come to know Jesus personally.
  • For our enemies and all who speak against us, that they may come to love Jesus, and that we may love them without hatred.

And in thanksgiving for every blessing You have already given, and for those who guide and care for us.

Surrender Novena (Don Dolindo Ruotolo) · Day 4 of 9

Day 4

You see evil growing instead of weakening? Do not worry. Close your eyes and say to me with faith: “Thy will be done, You take care of it.” I say to you that I will take care of it, and that I will intervene as does a doctor and I will accomplish miracles when they are needed. Do you see that the sick person is getting worse? Do not be upset, but close your eyes and say, “You take care of it.” I say to you that I will take care of it, and that there is no medicine more powerful than my loving intervention. By my love, I promise this to you.

Bible in a Year · Day 29
Where we are

Day 29 · Moses Returns to Egypt
Readings: Exodus 4–5, Leviticus 4, Psalm 46
Timeline period: Egypt and Exodus

Reflection

Day 29: Moses returns to Egypt. Moses objects that the people will not believe him and that he is slow of speech. God gives him signs, his staff becoming a serpent and his hand turning leprous and then healed. He gives him Aaron as his mouth, and sends him. Fr. Mike’s point is that God wants to set us free so that we can worship Him. Moses and Aaron gather the elders and do the signs, and the people believe and bow down in worship.

Notes
  • Catechism 2575. God calls Moses from the burning bush and makes him a model of prayer. Moses objects and asks questions, and in that conversation he learns to trust the God who sends him.
  • St. Gregory the Great, Pastoral Rule, Book I, ch. 7: Gregory says Moses rightly showed both humility and obedience: he did not want to be placed over so great a people, yet he obeyed when God’s will was clear.
  • Haydock on Exodus 4:10. God let Moses feel his weakness so that the glory of the work would be God’s, and so that Moses would stay humble while working wonders.
For prayer

What excuse am I giving God, and what if He has already given me the help I need?
