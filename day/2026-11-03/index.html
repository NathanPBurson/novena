Novena Opening Offering (in Thanksgiving)

Hail, Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, my Mother Mary, hail! At thy feet I gratefully kneel to offer thee a Crown of Roses, blood-red roses to remind thee of the passion of thy divine Son, with Whom thou didst so completely share each torment, each bud recalling to thee a holy mystery; each ten bound together with my thanksgiving for the favor I place in your hands. O Holy Queen, dispenser of God's graces, and Mother of all who invoke thee! Thou canst not look upon my gift and fail to see its binding. As thou receivest my gift, so wilt thou receive my thanksgiving; from thy bounty thou hast given me the favor I so earnestly and trustingly sought. I despaired not of what I asked of thee, and thou hast truly shown thyself my Mother!

PRAYER OF INTENTIONS

Lord Jesus, through the hands of Your Blessed Mother, I offer this Rosary for these intentions:

  • For this channel, that it may grow, bear good fruit, and become a way for me to serve You full-time.
  • For all our vocations, to the priesthood, religious life, or marriage, and for the safety and holiness of our future spouses and families.
  • For our stream family, all our lost sheep, and every viewer's private intentions, that they may grow in holiness and be protected.
  • For the sick, and for good health for our friends, families, and viewers.
  • For myself: for healing, for patience, fortitude, humility, and purity, and for a good, steady job.
  • For all who are suffering, struggling with addiction, despairing, or in need of hope.
  • For all who are far from God, and for those of other faiths, that they may come to know Jesus personally.
  • For our enemies and all who speak against us, that they may come to love Jesus, and that we may love them without hatred.

And in thanksgiving for every blessing You have already given, and for those who guide and care for us.

Surrender Novena (Don Dolindo Ruotolo) · Day 9 of 9

Day 9

Pray always in readiness to surrender, and you will receive from it great peace and great rewards, even when I confer on you the grace of immolation, of repentance, and of love. Then what does suffering matter? It seems impossible to you? Close your eyes and say with all your soul, “Jesus, you take care of it.” Do not be afraid, I will take care of things and you will bless my name by humbling yourself. A thousand prayers cannot equal one single act of surrender, remember this well. There is no novena more effective than this.

Ten times

O Jesus, I surrender myself to you, take care of everything!

To Our Lady

Mother, I am yours now and forever. Through you and with you I always want to belong completely to Jesus.

For men and beasts, and all things that will be found outside, not gathered in from the fields, and on which the hail will fall, shall die.’ ” 20He who feared the word of the Lord among the servants of Pharaoh caused his servants and cattle to flee together into the houses.

21But he who neglected the word of the Lord released his servants and cattle into the fields. 22And the Lord said to Moses: “Extend your hand into the sky, so that there may be hail in the entire land of Egypt, on men, and on beasts, and on every plant of the field in the land of Egypt.” 23And Moses extended his staff into the sky, and the Lord sent thunder and hail, and also lightning dashing across the earth. And the Lord rained down hail upon the land of Egypt. 24And the hail and intermingled fire drove on together. And the hail struck down every plant of the field, and it broke every tree of the region. 26Only in the land of Goshen, where the sons of Israel were, did the hail not fall. 27And Pharaoh sent and called Moses and Aaron, saying to them: “I have sinned even until now. 31And so, the flax and the barley were damaged, because the barley was growing, and the flax was already developing grains. 32But the wheat and the spelt were not damaged, because they were late. 33And Moses, departing from Pharaoh out of the city, reached out his hands toward the Lord. And the thunders and hail ceased, neither did there drop any more rain upon the land. 34Then Pharaoh, seeing that the rain, and the hail, and the thunders had ceased, added to his sin. 35And his heart was weighed down, along with that of his servants, and it was hardened exceedingly. Where we are

Day 32 · Cattle, Boils, and Hail
Readings: Exodus 9, Leviticus 7, Psalm 49
Timeline period: Egypt and Exodus

Reflection

Day 32: the episode summary says Fr. Mike uses Pharaoh to show how our hearts can either harden or melt in response to God. In Exodus 9 the Egyptians’ livestock die while Israel’s are untouched. Ashes from the oven become boils, so painful that the magicians cannot even stand before Moses. God tells Pharaoh he was raised up so that God’s strength and name would be known in all the earth. Before the hail, some of Pharaoh’s servants who feared the word of the Lord bring their servants and cattle indoors, while others leave them in the field. Pharaoh says, “I have sinned,” but when the thunder stops he sins again. The same events harden one heart and soften another.

Notes
  • Catechism 311. God is in no way, directly or indirectly, the cause of moral evil. He permits it, however, because he respects the freedom of his creatures and, mysteriously, knows how to derive good from it.
  • St. Augustine, City of God, Book XI, ch. 17: Augustine says God is the supremely good Creator of good natures and the most just Ruler of evil wills, so that He makes good use even of evil wills. Haydock cites this chapter on Exodus 9:16.
  • Haydock on Exodus 9:16. Haydock reads “raised thee” as God preserving Pharaoh until now, and says God arranges things so as to draw good out of the evil designs of men (citing Augustine, City of God XI.17, and Romans 9:17).
  • Haydock on Exodus 9:19. The warning to gather the cattle shows God tempering justice with mercy (Haydock cites St. Augustine, q. 33).
