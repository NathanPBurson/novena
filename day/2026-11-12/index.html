Novena Opening Offering (in Petition)

Hail, Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, my Mother Mary, hail! At thy feet I humbly kneel to offer thee a Crown of Roses, bright-yellow roses to remind thee of the light of thy Son's public life, each bud recalling to thee a holy mystery; each ten bound together with my petition for a particular grace. O Holy Queen, dispenser of God's graces, and Mother of all who invoke thee! Thou canst not look upon my gift and fail to see its binding. As thou receivest my gift, so wilt thou receive my petition; from thy bounty thou wilt give me the favor I so earnestly and trustingly seek: the favor I place in your hands. I despair of nothing that I ask of thee. Show thyself my Mother!

PRAYER OF INTENTIONS

Lord Jesus, through the hands of Your Blessed Mother, we offer this Rosary for these intentions — for all of us lost sheep together:

  • For all of us lost sheep, that our work and creative callings, including this channel, may bear fruit, and that each of us may find a fulfilling, steady career.
  • For all our vocations — to the priesthood, religious life, or marriage — and for the safety and holiness of our future spouses and families.
  • For our stream family, all our lost sheep, guests, fans, and viewers, and every private intention we carry, that we may grow in holiness and be protected.
  • For all of us, that we and our loved ones may receive healing in body and mind, and for good health for our friends, families, and all who watch with us.
  • For all of us: for healing, patience, fortitude, humility, and purity.
  • For all of us who are suffering, struggling with addiction, despairing, or in need of hope — that we may grow in freedom and purity of heart.
  • For all of us who are far from God, and for those of other faiths, that we may return to Christ and His Church and know Jesus personally.
  • For our enemies and all who speak against us, that they may come to love Jesus, and that we may love them without hatred.
  • For all of us, for discernment and freedom from all spiritual deception, that God in grace and mercy make the truth abundantly clear.
  • For protection and clarity for all of us to recognize bad advice, whether it comes from people or from AI.
  • For all of us, for the repose of the souls of our beloved dead.
  • For all of us, that peace and charity may grow in our families and homes.
  • For all of us, for our priests, bishops, and all who serve the Church.
  • For all of us, that we may clearly discern our vocations and future paths, and find peace amid anxiety, grief, and heavy burdens.
  • For all of us, that we may have what we need and be free from crushing financial worry, and have strength in our studies.
  • For all of us, for an end to suffering.
  • For all of us, for an end to pain.
  • For all of us, for an end to the feeling of loneliness.
  • For all of us, that we may be not just content but happy, and enjoy our lives while we wait to find our vocation.
  • For all of us, in thanksgiving for prayers answered and blessings received — and for every lost sheep watching and praying with us tonight.

And in thanksgiving for every blessing You have already given to all of us, and for those who guide and care for us.

And Aaron arrived with all the elders of Israel, in order to eat bread with him in the sight of God. 13Then, the next day, Moses sat down in order to judge the people, and they stood beside Moses from morning, even until evening. 14And when, of course, his kinsman saw all that he did among the people, he said: “What is this that you do among the people? The task is beyond your strength; you will not be able bear it alone. 19But listen to my words and counsels, and then God will be with you. Be available to the people in that which pertains to God, so as to refer what they say to him, 20and to reveal to the people the ceremonies, and the rituals of worship, and the way by which they should progress, and the work that they should do.

Then, when anything greater will have occurred, they may refer it to you, and let them judge the lesser matters only. But whatever was more serious, they referred to him, and they judged easier matters only. 27And he dismissed his kinsman, who, turning back, went to his own land.

Bible in a Year · Day 37
Where we are

Day 37 · The Mystical Works of God
Readings: Exodus 17–18, Leviticus 12, Psalm 73
Timeline period: Egypt and Exodus

Reflection

Still on Day 37’s horizon, Exodus 18 brings Jethro, who rejoices in what God has done, offers sacrifice, and then tells Moses the hard pastoral truth: you will wear yourself out if you judge alone. Shared governance is not unbelief; it is wisdom under God.

Commentary & notes
  • Catechism 1897. Human society needs authority. Authority is exercised legitimately when it seeks the common good by morally upright means — Jethro’s counsel in seed form.
  • St. Bede, commentary tradition on Exodus leadership: Commentators praise Jethro’s humility and Moses’ teachability: a pagan priest can speak prudence, and the prophet can listen.
  • Haydock on Exodus 18:17-18. “The thing is not good you are doing… You will be consumed.” Even holy zeal needs structure.
  • Haydock on Exodus 18:21. Able men, fearing God, truthful, hating bribes — the job description for shared judgment still preaches.
  • Today's Mass (USCCB, Nov 12 2026). Memorial of St. Josaphat. Luke 17:20–25 — the Kingdom is among you. Jethro’s order and Josaphat’s martyrdom for unity both serve a Kingdom already near.
  • St. Josaphat. Bishop and martyr for communion with Peter. Exodus 18 orders a people; Josaphat dies so that order may be communion, not fracture.
Joan’s note

Jethro invents middle management and saves the prophet’s back. If even Moses needed a org chart, your to-do list can survive a delegate button.

For prayer

Where is God sending a Jethro to keep me from burning out — and am I humble enough to listen?

  • He tells Moses to appoint capable men who fear God, love truth, and hate avarice, as tribunes and leaders of hundreds, fifties, and tens.
