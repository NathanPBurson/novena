Novena Opening Offering (in Petition)

Hail, Queen of the Most Holy Rosary, my Mother Mary, hail! At thy feet I humbly kneel to offer thee a Crown of Roses, snow-white buds to remind thee of thy joys, each bud recalling to thee a holy mystery; each ten bound together with my petition for a particular grace. O Holy Queen, dispenser of God's graces, and Mother of all who invoke thee! Thou canst not look upon my gift and fail to see its binding. As thou receivest my gift, so wilt thou receive my petition; from thy bounty thou wilt give me the favor I so earnestly and trustingly seek: the favor I place in your hands. I despair of nothing that I ask of thee. Show thyself my Mother!

PRAYER OF INTENTIONS

Lord Jesus, through the hands of Your Blessed Mother, we offer this Rosary for these intentions — for all of us lost sheep together:

  • For all of us lost sheep, that our work and creative callings, including this channel, may bear fruit, and that each of us may find a fulfilling, steady career.
  • For all our vocations — to the priesthood, religious life, or marriage — and for the safety and holiness of our future spouses and families.
  • For our stream family, all our lost sheep, guests, fans, and viewers, and every private intention we carry, that we may grow in holiness and be protected.
  • For all of us, that we and our loved ones may receive healing in body and mind, and for good health for our friends, families, and all who watch with us.
  • For all of us: for healing, patience, fortitude, humility, and purity.
  • For all of us who are suffering, struggling with addiction, despairing, or in need of hope — that we may grow in freedom and purity of heart.
  • For all of us who are far from God, and for those of other faiths, that we may return to Christ and His Church and know Jesus personally.
  • For our enemies and all who speak against us, that they may come to love Jesus, and that we may love them without hatred.
  • For all of us, for discernment and freedom from all spiritual deception, that God in grace and mercy make the truth abundantly clear.
  • For protection and clarity for all of us to recognize bad advice, whether it comes from people or from AI.
  • For all of us, for the repose of the souls of our beloved dead.
  • For all of us, that peace and charity may grow in our families and homes.
  • For all of us, for our priests, bishops, and all who serve the Church.
  • For all of us, that we may clearly discern our vocations and future paths, and find peace amid anxiety, grief, and heavy burdens.
  • For all of us, that we may have what we need and be free from crushing financial worry, and have strength in our studies.
  • For all of us, for an end to suffering.
  • For all of us, for an end to pain.
  • For all of us, for an end to the feeling of loneliness.
  • For all of us, that we may be not just content but happy, and enjoy our lives while we wait to find our vocation.
  • For all of us, in thanksgiving for prayers answered and blessings received — and for every lost sheep watching and praying with us tonight.

And in thanksgiving for every blessing You have already given to all of us, and for those who guide and care for us.

Surrender Novena (Don Dolindo Ruotolo) · Day 2 of 9

Day 2

Surrender to me does not mean to fret, to be upset, or to lose hope, nor does it mean offering to me a worried prayer asking me to follow you and change your worry into prayer. It is against this surrender, deeply against it, to worry, to be nervous and to desire to think about the consequences of anything. It is like the confusion that children feel when they ask their mother to see to their needs, and then try to take care of those needs for themselves so that their childlike efforts get in their mother’s way. Surrender means to placidly close the eyes of the soul, to turn away from thoughts of tribulation and to put yourself in my care, so that only I act, saying, “You take care of it.”

Ten times

O Jesus, I surrender myself to you, take care of everything!

To Our Lady

Mother, I am yours now and forever. Through you and with you I always want to belong completely to Jesus.

You shall not do any work in it: you and your son and your daughter, your male servant and your female servant, your beast and the newcomer who is within your gates. 11For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, and the sea, and all the things that are in them, and so he rested on the seventh day. For this reason, the Lord has blessed the day of the Sabbath and sanctified it. 12Honor your father and your mother, so that you may have a long life upon the land, which the Lord your God will give to you. 13You shall not murder. 14You shall not commit adultery. 15You shall not steal. 16You shall not speak false testimony against your neighbor. 17You shall not covet the house of your neighbor; neither shall you desire his wife, nor male servant, nor female servant, nor ox, nor donkey, nor anything that is his.” 18Then all the people considered the voices, and the lights, and the sound of the trumpet, and the smoking mountain. And being terrified and struck with fear, they stood at a distance, 19saying to Moses: “Speak to us, and we will listen. Let not the Lord speak to us, lest perhaps we may die.” 20And Moses said to the people: “Do not be afraid. For God came in order to test you, and so that the dread of him might be with you, and you would not sin.” 21And the people stood far away. But Moses approached toward the mist, in which was God. 22Thereafter, the Lord said to Moses: “This you shall say to the sons of Israel: You have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven. 23You shall not make gods of silver, nor shall you make for yourselves gods of gold. 24You shall make an altar from the earth for me, and you shall offer upon it your holocausts and peace-offerings, your sheep and oxen, in every place where the memory of my name shall be. I will come to you, and I will bless you. 25And if you make an altar of stone for me, you shall not build it from cut stones; for if you lift up a tool over it, it will be defiled. 26You shall not ascend by steps to my altar, lest your nakedness be revealed.”
